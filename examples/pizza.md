Pizza margherita for 2
======================

A classic thin-crust pizza with a quick no-cook tomato sauce. The dough
needs an hour to rise, so give yourself ninety minutes start to finish.

## Make the dough and sauce

    "preheat oven to its hottest setting (250°C / 480°F+) and slide in a baking stone or upturned tray" (_oven)

    300g strong white bread flour
    7g fast-action dried yeast
    1/2 tsp caster sugar
    200ml warm water
    1 tbsp olive oil
    400g chopped tomatoes
    2 cloves garlic, crushed
    1/2 tsp dried oregano
    125g mozzarella, torn
    "a few basil leaves"

    dough := "mix, knead for 10 minutes and leave to rise for 1 hr" (flour, yeast, sugar, warm water, olive oil)
    pizza sauce := "stir together and season to taste" (chopped tomatoes, garlic, oregano)

## Assemble and bake

    "knock back the dough, roll out thin, top and bake for 8–10 minutes" (dough, pizza sauce, mozzarella, basil leaves)

Rest the rolled dough for five minutes before topping if it keeps shrinking
back. Finish with a grind of black pepper and a slick of good olive oil.
