Sunday feast for 8
==================

A deliberately enormous recipe for testing pagination.

    "take the butter and eggs out of the fridge to come up to temperature" (_fridge)
    "preheat oven to 180°C / 350°F" (_oven)
    "line two baking trays with parchment" (_trays)

    500g plain flour
    250g cold butter, cubed
    1 tsp fine salt
    2 tbsp caster sugar
    6 egg yolks
    4 tbsp iced water
    1kg cooking apples
    100g sultanas
    2 tsp ground cinnamon
    50g demerara sugar
    200g mascarpone
    1 vanilla pod
    2 tbsp icing sugar
    500ml double cream
    300g blackberries
    1 lemon, zested
    100g flaked almonds
    2 tbsp apricot jam
    1kg beef shin, diced
    2 onions, sliced
    4 carrots, chunked
    500ml red wine
    500ml beef stock
    4 bay leaves

    pastry := "rub together, rest for 1 hr" (flour, butter, salt, caster sugar, egg yolks, iced water)
    filling := "stew until soft, then cool" (apples, sultanas, cinnamon, demerara sugar)
    custard := "infuse, whisk and chill" (mascarpone, vanilla pod, icing sugar, double cream)
    compote := "simmer until jammy" (blackberries, lemon)
    stew := "brown then braise for 3 hrs" (beef shin, onions, carrots, red wine, beef stock, bay leaves)

    "roll, fill and crimp the pies, brush with leftover egg white and bake for 35 min" (pastry, filling, apricot jam, flaked almonds)
