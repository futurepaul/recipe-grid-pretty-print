Breakfast muffins for 12
========================

Make-ahead breakfast muffins: eggs and bacon baked onto English muffins.
Wrap them individually and they keep for a week in the fridge or a month in
the freezer.

    "preheat oven to 300°F / 150°C" (_oven)
    "generously oil a rimmed half sheet pan (13″ × 18″)" (_pan)

    12 eggs
    1/4 cup whole milk
    1 1/2 tsp salt
    {6 slices} bacon
    "a few handfuls spinach"
    12 english muffins
    "cheese (optional)"
    "butter (optional)"

    whisked = "whisk eggs, milk & salt" (eggs, whole milk, salt)
    fried = "fry bacon until crispy, add spinach, wilt & drain" (bacon, "a few handfuls spinach")
    stirred = "stir bacon & spinach into egg mixture" (whisked, fried)
    baked = "pour into pan, bake 300°F / 150°C, 15 min" (stirred)
    "cool, cut into rounds, assemble on muffins" (baked, english muffins, "cheese (optional)", "butter (optional)")

To serve, reheat in a microwave for around a minute, or toast the muffin
halves in a fry pan until crisp.
